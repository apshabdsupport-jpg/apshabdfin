# HSR Layout — design research

## Core identity

HSR is short for Hosur–Sarjapur Road Layout. It was planned by the Bangalore Development Authority in the mid-1980s as a seven-sector grid. Its strongest present-day identity is not a single monument, but the collision of civic planning, broad numbered roads, parks and Agara Lake with startup, food, fitness and young creative culture.

## Visual anchors used in D1 back-v3

- Seven numbered sectors presented as a BDA plot-allotment plan.
- 27th Main and 19th Main as the yellow cross/spine of the layout.
- “Hosur × Sarjapur” as the origin of the neighbourhood name.
- NIFT Sector 1 expressed through garment pattern paper, cut lines and a pattern ruler.
- Agara-side morning life using the supplied lake photograph.
- 27th Main evening culture using the supplied café-and-chess photograph.
- A BMTC-style bus and Bengaluru auto as everyday movement, without official logos.
- Coordinates 12.91°N, 77.64°E and postcode 560102 as civic identifiers.

## Direction

The design phrase is: “A plotted system that became a culture.” The visual language combines municipal blueprint, plot paperwork, street signage, fashion pattern-cutting and personal-photo inserts. It should feel like a document from HSR rather than a generic illustrated neighbourhood souvenir.
