### Reach risk — read before you hit post

Uncensored Hindi gaalis in the caption are the point, but Instagram's caption filter can throttle distribution or block the post from Explore and hashtag surfaces. Three ways to play it, pick one:

1. **Ship as written.** Highest bite. Accept the reach gamble. Recommended if you care more about the people who screenshot it than the algorithm.
2. **Censor only the two hardest words in the caption, keep them uncensored in the comments.** Change `"BHENCHOD"` → `"BHENC**D"` and `chutiya` → `chu*iya` in the caption. Post the full uncensored line as your own first comment. Comments are policed far more loosely.
3. **Ad-safe swap** (only if this will ever be boosted): replace the opening two lines with —
   `SAY IT IN ENGLISH AND IT'S EDGY. SAY IT IN HINDI AND IT'S "SANSKAAR KHARAB."`
   and cut `gaandu` from the CP line.

The middle-finger image itself is fine — it is stylised, no nudity, no violence.

## Note on the hook

The opening is doing real work, not just being loud: the observation is that Indians have made English profanity aspirational and Hindi profanity shameful, which is the same class reflex that makes BROOKLYN sell and BHANDUP embarrassing. Everything after it — the area roast, the product line — is that one idea applied. If you edit anything, don't edit the first two lines.
