## Shared art direction

Create a cohesive APSHABD punk-zine streetwear campaign from the supplied project photography and official wordmark. Use tactile torn bone paper, near-black photocopy fields, vermilion spray paint, Indian newspaper and transport-ticket fragments, masking tape, staples, halftone grain, registration marks, and xerox shadows. The result should feel filthy, premium, confrontational, unmistakably Indian, and readable on a phone. Preserve people, city-shirt lettering, and the APSHABD wordmark. Avoid glossy corporate design, generic western punk symbols, skulls, anarchy logos, rainbow palettes, and watermarks.
